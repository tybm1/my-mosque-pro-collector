Our Services For The Community
Darus Salam is a multi-purpose community center designed to offer a range of locally based projects to meet the needs of the local community it serves.
Prayer Services
Darus Salam Mosque is open 7 days a week and 365 days a year, for people to use our prayer facilities.
Children Madrasah
Our classes are offered to young Muslims between the aged of 6 and 17 years.
Arabic Language
An opportunity to read, write and understand Arabic is offered, in pre-arranged lessons.
Tafseer Class
Offered in Bangla and English every Tuesday after the Zuhr Salah.
Islamic Marriages
Darus Salam Mosque is recognised as an approved venue for marriage registrations
Study Circle
Study circles are run to encourage learning through mutual dialogue between the participants of the circle.
