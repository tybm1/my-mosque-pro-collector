Prayer Times | Central Mosque Rochdale
🕌 Eid News
✕
Assalaamu Alaikum,
The Rochdale Council of Mosques announces that due to positive moon sightings the month of Ramadhan will end 29 days and the 1st of Shawwal 1447AH -
Eid al Fitr - will be on Friday 20th March 2026
Inshaa Allah
Eid Salaah will take place at the Eidgah Musallah Inshaa Allah
Springfield Park - 10:30am
May Allah SWT accept everyone's efforts throughout this blessed month
📢
This year Eid Salah will be at the Eidgah in Springifeld, Rochdale - Pre-Salaah Talk: 10:00 AM /
Eid Salaah: 10:30 AM (sharp)
🕌 Tarawīḥ Salah Venues: Ramadān 1447
✕

Central Masjid Rochdale 2nd Floor | Darul Ilm Classrooms | Early Tarawīḥ CCC
Khatmul Qur'ān 29 th Ramadān | Khatmul Qur'ān 27 th Ramadān | Khatmul Qur'ān 25 th Ramadān | Khatmul Qur'ān 20 th Ramadān | Khatmul Qur'ān 23rd Ramadān | –

Riwāyah of Imām Dhakwān R.A.
as narrated from
