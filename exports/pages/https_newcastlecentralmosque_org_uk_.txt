Welcome to Newcastle Central Mosque
Established in 1970, Newcastle Central Mosque is one of Newcastle upon Tyne’s oldest and largest mosques. Our ethos is to provide a lifelong learning and development opportunity alongside addressing the social and spiritual needs of Muslims, helping them positively contribute to the diverse Britain we live in today.
