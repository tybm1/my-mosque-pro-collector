Welcome to Bow Central Mosque
Bow Central Mosque was founded in 1998 and has a unique origin. The venue had been a local pub for many years and was finally purchased in a vandalised state to be converted to a Mosque.
		  Very quickly, the old pub had transformed into the community's much needed social and religious center. Since then, Bow Central Mosque has been serving the community through not only five-daily prayers, but also facilitating many other socio-cultural activities.
Registered Charity No. 1108433
