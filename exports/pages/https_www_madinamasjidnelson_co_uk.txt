Welcome to Madina Masjid Nelson
The Madina Masjid Nelson is not just a mosque for prayers rather it is a community Hub for all. Whilst our Centre is committed to preserving an Islamic identity, we focus to building and supporting Muslim's as well as people from all religious backgrounds and none, in our community, promoting a peace love and mercy as taught from the Islamic way of life based on the Holy Quran and the Sunnah (Traditions) of Prophet Muhammad, peace and blessings be upon him.
