Abu Zahra Charity
The Foundation is dedicated to supporting needy individuals locally and across the globe, providing essential aid, food, and medical support to those most in need. We work closely with trusted local charities on the ground, ensuring effective and timely assistance at key flashpoints around the Muslim world. Our mission is rooted in compassion, dignity, and impactful service.
