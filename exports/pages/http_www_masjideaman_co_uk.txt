MASJID-E-AMAN
Masjid-E-Aman was founded in 1979 by the late Abdus Sabur Choudhury. Initially setup as a home office, whereby people could acquire free advice on personal matters.
As the early Bangladeshi settlers arrived in Birmingham, our centre proved to be a vital source of assistance.
Later it was turned into a place of worship for the local muslim community, at a time when there were no other facilities available for them.
Since than it has gone from strength to strength. It began providing education and support for those in need.
Now it has branched even further, with a vision to give to those who are less fortunate, through the Suleman Trust Charity.
