About Us
Madani Education Centre is dedicated to promoting the authentic teachings of Islam to the entire community. We aim to provide high-quality Islamic education for children aged 5+, as well as learning opportunities for adults.
Madani Education Centre was established in 2009. It started off with a small number of musallees and has now grown substantially. We currently have more than 50 musallees attending salah daily. and more than 150 musallees for Jumu’aah salah.
